Ingredients & Allergens

Korean cuisine heavily incorporates sesame, soy, and gluten. Please check details below:

Common Seasonings

Dishes utilize soy sauce (gluten/soy), sesame oil/seeds, garlic, green onions, and red pepper paste (gochujang).

Allergen Summary

  • Soy / Gluten: Present in Bulgogi, Gangjeong, Japchae, Gimbap, Bibimbap, Mandoo, Tteok-ko-chi, and Sso-tteok.
  • Sesame: Present in Bulgogi, Gimbap, Japchae, Bibimbap, Mandoo, Tteok-ko-chi, and Sso-tteok.
  • Fish Sauce: In Kimchi.
  • Egg: In Gimbap (and optional on Bibimbap).

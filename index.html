Ingredients & Safety

Key Korean Seasonings

Dishes contain sesame oil, soy sauce (gluten), garlic, green onion, and fermented red pepper paste (gochujang).

Allergen Quick Guide

  • Gluten / Soy: In Bulgogi, Gangjeong, Japchae, Gimbap, Bibimbap, Mandoo, and Tteok-ko-chi.
  • Sesame (oil/seeds): Used in Bulgogi, Gimbap, Japchae, Bibimbap, Mandoo, and Tteok-ko-chi.
  • Fish Sauce: Present in Kimchi.
  • Egg: In Gimbap and optional on Bibimbap.

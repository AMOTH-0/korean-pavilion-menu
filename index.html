Ingredients & Allergens

We believe in serving fresh, honest food. Here is a guide to the core ingredients used in our dishes. Please let our servers know of any allergies before ordering.

Common Korean Condiments

Our dishes owe their deep, complex flavors to traditional Korean seasonings, including:

  • Gochujang (고추장): Thick, sweet, and spicy fermented red chili paste. Used in Chicken Gangjeong, Tteok-ko-chi, and Bibimbap.
  • Sesame Oil (참기름): Rich, toasted oil that provides a nutty aroma. Used in Bulgogi, Gimbap, Japchae, and Bibimbap.
  • Garlic & Green Onion (마늘과 파): The foundation of almost all marinades and seasonings.
  • Fish Sauce (액젓): Provides savory, umami depths in our Kimchi.

Allergen Quick Guide

Due to the traditional recipes of Korean cuisine, please note the following allergen references:

Soy / Gluten Present in soy sauce used in Bulgogi, Gangjeong, Japchae, Gimbap, Bibimbap, Mandoo, and Tteok-ko-chi.
Sesame Used as oil or garnish seeds on Bulgogi, Gimbap, Japchae, Bibimbap, Mandoo, and Tteok-ko-chi.
Fish / Seafood Kimchi contains fish sauce. Mandoo wraps and filling are vegetarian, but check with staff regarding shared preparation.
Egg Used in traditional Gimbap filling and optional on Bibimbap.
